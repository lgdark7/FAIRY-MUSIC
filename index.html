Security & Integrity

Verify Your APK Before Installing

Because Fairy Music is distributed directly as an APK rather than through Google Play Store, every release is cryptographically signed with our official developer keystore.

Fairy Music includes an in-app AppSecurityGuard module that validates the APK signing certificate on startup, preventing tampered or repackaged APKs from executing.

Installation Guide

How to Install on Android

Installing an APK on modern Android devices takes less than 30 seconds.

STEP 01

Download APK

Tap the download button to save the official .apk file to your device.

STEP 02

Open File

Open your browser's download drawer or device Files manager and tap the downloaded APK.

STEP 03

Allow Unknown Apps

If prompted by Android, toggle "Allow from this source" for your browser in Settings.

STEP 04

Install & Enjoy

Tap "Install". The in-app updater will notify you automatically when new updates release!
